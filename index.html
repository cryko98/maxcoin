01

genesis

How a punchline acquired a hash function.

  1. dec 2013

    the bet

    Max Keiser, host of the Keiser Report, jokes on Twitter that if a coin carrying his name ever reaches a billion dollar market cap, he will present the show naked. He is not writing a specification. Someone reads it as one.

  2. jan 2014

    the build

    Jordan Fish — @cobie — and Luke Mitchell take it literally and fork Bitcoin. SHA-256 comes out, Keccak goes in. Schnorr signatures turn up in the descriptions. A celebrity coin, years before anybody used that phrase without wincing.

  3. 28 jan 2014

    episode 555

    Keiser Report 555, titled Launch of Maxcoin. The genesis block is mined on air by the man the coin is named after. No presale. No deck. A studio, a camera, and a hash landing in real time.

  4. 2014 →

    the forum years

    Thread 438150 on Bitcointalk. r/maxcoinproject on Reddit. Pool operators, block explorers, miners, arguments, a logo. The paper trail was never taken down and you can still read all of it.

  5. now

    back onchain

    $MAXCOIN on Base. Same name, same joke, different chain. Everything on this page that is history can be checked in the archive below; everything that is a token can be checked onchain.

03

episode 555

The only token launch that needed a wardrobe clause.

A billion dollar market cap and I do the show naked.

the tweet, paraphrased — the one the fork was built on

Every launch since has been a livestream, a countdown, a bonding curve. This one was broadcast television. The host mined the first block of a chain named after himself, live, while explaining on air what a block was.

The market cap condition was never met. The wardrobe was never tested. The chain kept producing blocks anyway, which is more than most launches from that year managed.
